PRINCE OF PERSIA - LEVEL 1: QUICK START

Windows x64. Extract the game ZIP first, then open PrinceOfPersia-Level1.exe.
Press Enter to begin. No installation or administrator privileges are required.
Keep the manual and license files alongside the game.

MOVE:     Left/Right run; Up jumps/climbs; Down crouches.
CAREFUL:  Stop, then hold Shift and tap Left/Right. Release between steps.
LEDGES:   Hold Shift to catch/stay hanging. Keep Shift held and press Up to climb.
ITEMS:    Face the sword/potion and tap Shift nearby. Try crouching if needed.
FIGHT:    Collect the sword first. Tap Shift to strike; tap Up to parry.
          Advance/retreat with the arrows. Down puts the sword away.
GATES:    Step on the linked floor plate. Some plates close gates instead.
EXIT:     Reach the opened final doorway and press Up. Level one ends here.

Enter/Space = pause/resume    R = new game    M = mute    Escape = quit
The game pauses on focus loss. Click back and press Enter to resume.
Original music pauses the action and finishes automatically.

Full moves: CONTROLS.txt

The native game does not require Python, Conda, WSL, Codex, REA or an emulator.
This is an unofficial reconstruction of the first level of the 1989 Apple II game.
