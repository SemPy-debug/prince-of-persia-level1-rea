PRINCE OF PERSIA - LEVEL 1: CONTROLS FOR THIS BUILD

MOVEMENT
  Run / turn          Hold Left or Right. Release to stop.
  Careful step        Stop running first. Hold Shift and tap Left or Right.
                      Release the arrow between steps; Shift is not a brake.
  Jump straight up    Up.
  Standing jump       From a stop, press Up + Left or Up + Right together.
  Running jump        Build up a run with Left/Right, keep that direction
                      held, then press Up before the gap.
  Crouch              Hold Down. Release to stand.
  Crouched step       Keep Down held, then tap the direction he is facing.
                      Turn around before crouching if you need to go back.

LEDGES
  Climb up            Face a reachable ledge and press Up.
  Catch a ledge       Hold Shift while jumping/falling within reach.
  Keep hanging        Keep Shift held.
  Pull yourself up    While hanging, keep Shift held and press Up.
  Let go              Release Up, then release Shift while hanging.
  Climb down          Carefully approach an edge, turn to face away from
                      the drop, then press Down. Hold Shift to hang.

ITEMS AND EXIT
  Drink / collect     Face the potion or sword and tap Shift nearby.
                      If he only kneels, hold Down and tap Shift again
                      once he is crouched. Release Shift between presses.
  Open gates          Step on the appropriate floor plate. Some close gates;
                      others open them. There is no separate use-gate key.
  Finish the level    Reach the opened exit and press Up at the doorway.

SWORD FIGHTING - AFTER COLLECTING THE SWORD
  Draw                Usually automatic near a guard; Shift draws it again
                      when an opponent is close enough.
  Strike              Tap Shift. Release, then press again for another blow.
  Block / parry       Tap Up as the guard's attack comes toward you.
  Counterattack       After a successful block, tap Shift.
  Advance             Tap the direction you are facing, toward the guard.
  Retreat             Tap the opposite direction.
  Put sword away      Down. Movement then returns to running/jumping.

WINDOW AND GAME
  Enter or Space      Pause / resume.
  R                   Start level one again as a new game.
  M                   Mute / unmute.
  Escape              Quit.
  Resize window       Drag the window border.

TIPS
  You begin without a sword. Find and collect it before fighting the guard.
  Animation timing matters: let a turn or landing settle before the next
  move. A standing jump is shorter than a running jump. Hold Shift during
  a doubtful jump so the Prince can catch the far ledge if it is reachable.
  Releasing Shift while hanging makes him drop.
  Original music pauses the action and finishes automatically.
  Changing window focus pauses the game; click back and press Enter.
  This build ends after level one.
